Iconography

One library everywhere: Material Symbols Rounded, from Google Fonts, at weight 400. Outlined at rest, filled when active or inside a token.

The rule is simple: if it can be tapped, named or counted, it has an icon. Navigation, every asset class, every kind of account, every activity type, every status and every line of evidence.

Icons are drawn at 24 px on a 20 px optical grid and never scaled below 16 px. They take the colour of their text.
