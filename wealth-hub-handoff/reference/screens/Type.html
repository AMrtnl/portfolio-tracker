Typography

Instrument Serif speaks. It is used for headlines and the wordmark only, in sentence case, with tight leading and never in bold.

DM Sans counts. It sets everything that is read or added up: body text, interface labels and every figure, with tabular numerals so columns line up.

Two sizes of contrast are enough: a large serif line and small, quiet sans around it.
