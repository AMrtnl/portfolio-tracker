Data graphics

Two registers. The big charts, the ones a screen is built around, are drawn smooth and rounded so they read calmly. The small ones, sparklines, meters and creep bars, are drawn on the pixel grid of the pictures, where a little texture adds life without costing legibility.

Ultramarine is the measure. Green and red appear only for money in and money out, gain and loss. Gold marks today. Forecasts are the same shape at lower strength on a marble band, never a different colour.

Use the pixel only where a chart is small and secondary. If a chart needs a paragraph to explain it, it is the wrong chart.
