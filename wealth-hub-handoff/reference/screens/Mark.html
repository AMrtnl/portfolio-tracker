Logomark

The mark is a temple seen from the front left, engraved. It is cut in horizontal lines that swell where the stone is in shadow and thin out where it catches the light, the way banknotes, stamps and share certificates have always been drawn. The roof alone is gold.

It is one drawing in four weights, so it stays legible at any size: 62 lines from 260 px up, 46 lines down to 110 px, 24 lines down to 48 px, and 15 heavy lines below that. The weight changes by itself with the size.

It is vector at every weight. There is no tile, no outline and no container behind it. It sits directly on the ground.
