Tokens and logos

A token is the tile at the start of every row. A class token is a tinted tile with a filled icon, for things with no brand: a flat, a pension, a loan.

A logo token holds the real logo, in its own colour, on a tile tinted with that colour, with a class badge on the corner. In these designs the logos come from Simple Icons, an open CC0 set. In the app they load at run time from a logo service, by ticker, ISIN or domain.

Where no open logo exists, as for most banks and fund houses, the tile shows a monogram in the brand colour. The layout never changes, so a missing logo never breaks a row.
