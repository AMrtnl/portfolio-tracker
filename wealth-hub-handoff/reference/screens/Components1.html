The interface is made of the same things as the brand: marble ground, white cards with generous corners, ultramarine for the one thing to do next, and an icon on everything you can touch.

Radii step with size: 999 for controls, 28 for cards, 16 for fields and icon tiles. Touch targets never drop below 44 px.

Gold is kept for the figure that matters: a saving, the end of the net worth line.
