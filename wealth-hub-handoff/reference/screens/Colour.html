Colour

The palette is the hero picture reduced to five colours: marble cream, the ultramarine of the sky, the night of the foreground, sun-warmed stone and olive.

Cream carries most surfaces. Ultramarine is the brand colour. Stone is an accent, used as sparingly as the gold roof on the mark.

In the app, green means gain and red means owed, cost or risk. Neither is a brand colour.
